<h4>Purposes</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        <strong>Relationships Between Continuous Variables: </strong> Understand the relationships between pairs of variables in multivariate data and examine how to visualize the relationships and compute statistics that describe their associations.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Comparing Groups Tables and Visualizations:</strong> Use descriptive summaries by groups and visualize them to investigate differences between groups.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Comparing Groups Statistical Tests:</strong> Understand the use of statistical tests to identify differences between groups in data.
    </li>
</ol>
