<h4>Purposes</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        <strong>Welcome to R:</strong> Deliver essential knowledge within a minimal timeframe by employing hands-on learning techniques to enhance productivity in the R programming language.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>An Overview of the R Language:</strong> Equip beginners with a programming foundation by leveraging the R language, enabling practical application in marketing research and analytics.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Describing Data:</strong> Utilize descriptive statistics and single variable visualization techniques for summarizing and exploring a data set.
    </li>
</ol>
